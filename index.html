Betul Orcan-Ekmekci, Ph.D.

ASSOCIATE TEACHING PROFESSOR OF MATHEMATICS

Rice University

I am a mathematician and educator in the Department of Mathematics at Rice University, where I have taught since 2011 and served as an Associate Teaching Professor since 2023. My research focuses on nonlinear partial differential equations, free boundary problems, fluid dynamics, and homogenization.

Teaching Research CV

Teaching

Enter with curiosity. Leave with confidence.

Teaching and mentoring undergraduate students are central to my work at Rice. I aim to create an environment where students feel comfortable asking questions, exploring ideas, making connections, and developing confidence as independent mathematical thinkers.

I coordinate Rice Mathematics' Directed Reading Program, which pairs undergraduates with graduate student and faculty mentors for independent study projects, and I advise undergraduate research.

Research

My research focuses on nonlinear partial differential equations and their applications, including free boundary problems, fluid dynamics, homogenization, and mathematical modeling.

Nonlinear PDEs

Analysis of nonlinear partial differential equations and their applications.

Free Boundary Problems

Geometry, regularity, and homogenization of free boundary problems.

Fluid Dynamics

Mathematical analysis and modeling of fluid systems.

Homogenization

Stationary ergodic and layered homogenization for free boundary problems.

Mathematics Education

Teaching, mentoring, and innovative approaches to undergraduate mathematics.
