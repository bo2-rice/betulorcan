Directed Reading Program

Pairing undergraduates with mentors for independent study in mathematics.

Are you interested in reading math with some math enthusiasts like you?

You can join the Directed Reading Program every semester, including summer. Each project pairs an undergraduate with a graduate student or faculty mentor for an independent study of a mathematical topic chosen together, culminating in a short presentation.
