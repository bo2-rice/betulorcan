Supervised research projects in mathematics and fluid dynamics.

Fluid Dynamics — Summer 2019

In Summer 2019, Chumeng and Ethan worked on partial differential equations — specifically the Laplace Equation, Transport Equation, and Euler's Equation from fluid dynamics. Along the way, they mastered LaTeX for their report and poster preparation. They presented their research in Spring 2020 at the Wiess School of Natural Sciences reception.

Us from our last meeting :) After meeting eight weeks, three hours every weekday — either we were still happy, or we were happy it was over :) It was a pleasure working with these two mathematicians.

I'd like to thank my Mathematics department for supporting this research.
